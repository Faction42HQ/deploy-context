# Audience & Communication

## Who Deploy is for
Crypto-native users comfortable with self-custodial wallets and on-chain perps who want
autonomous, strategy-driven trading without surrendering custody — ranging from
yield-seekers (Income) to those wanting directional exposure (Superstar).

## How to communicate about Deploy
- **Lead with custody and autonomy** — the differentiators: keep your keys, the agent does the work.
- **Be precise, not hypey.** This is finance/crypto; exact, sober language builds trust.
- **Specifics over adjectives.** Explain the mechanism (session keys, funding rates), not "revolutionary."
- **Always pair opportunity with risk.** Never imply guaranteed returns.

## Compliance (hard rules — override tone and growth goals)
1. **No personalized financial or investment advice.** Educate; do not tell an individual what to do with their money.
2. **No performance or return claims without a sourced, current figure** from the docs or official data.
3. **Always honor risk disclosures.** Trading perps carries risk, including loss.
4. When a claim is sensitive or unverifiable, **flag it for human review** rather than publishing.
