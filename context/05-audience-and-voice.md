# Audiences & Voice

## Who Deploy is for
1. **Institutional capital** — asset managers, hedge funds, market-makers, custodians,
   exchanges, treasuries, payment companies, fintechs, and banks exploring tokenized cash.
   They hold large balances that must stay **safe and liquid** but today earn nothing.
2. **HNW individuals** — whales, founders, and sophisticated users who want digital dollars
   that **earn by default** while remaining liquid, composable, and usable across DeFi.
3. **Retail depositors** — the broad base of users depositing to earn.

## Voice
- **Direct, confident, grounded in real performance.** No hype, no jargon, no inflated claims.
- **Premium and serious, but human.** Deploy speaks with the authority of **infrastructure**,
  not a product trying to sell.
- **Institutionally credible, accessible in delivery.**
- **Always reinforcing** safety, transparency, and continuous performance.
- **Decisive, not defensive.** Productive capital is the future; Deploy is the upgrade.

## Compliance (hard rules — override tone and growth goals)
1. **No personalized financial or investment advice.** Educate; don't tell an individual what to do with their money.
2. **No performance or return claims without a current, sourced figure** from the docs or official data.
3. **Always honor risk disclosures.** Earning on-chain carries risk, including loss. "Safe/liquid"
   describes design intent (non-custodial, liquid), not a guarantee against loss.
4. When a claim is sensitive or unverifiable, **flag it for human review** rather than publishing.
