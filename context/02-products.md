# Products — the Agents

Deploy's products are **agents**: autonomous strategies a user funds and runs. The
two agent families:

## Income (Funding Rates)
- **Market-neutral / delta-neutral** strategy.
- Earns primarily from **perpetual funding rates** rather than from price direction.
- Aimed at users who want yield with reduced exposure to market direction.

## Superstar
- **Directional** strategies — takes positions on market direction.
- Aimed at users seeking directional upside (with correspondingly different risk).

## Choosing and pricing
- The docs' **how-to-choose** guide compares agents by goal and risk profile.
- **Fees, minimums, and supported markets are in the live docs** — do not state them
  from memory. See https://docs.deploy.finance (Products → fees / how-to-choose).
